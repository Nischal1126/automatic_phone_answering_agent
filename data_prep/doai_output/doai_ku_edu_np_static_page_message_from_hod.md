#  Message from HoD 
## Message from the In-Charge
I am honored to serve as the Incharge of the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Kathmandu University. At a time of rapid technological change, I look forward to working closely with our dedicated faculty, passionate students, and valued partners to make a real difference through AI.
Our goal is simple: to build a strong, forward-thinking AI community where students gain practical skills, where research solves real problems, and where ideas turn into solutions that benefit society. We believe AI should be learned with purpose, developed responsibly, and used to improve lives.
Together, let’s continue growing a department where innovation thrives, learning is meaningful, and everyone has the chance to contribute and succeed in the world of AI.
Amrit Dahal
In-Charge, Department of Artificial Intelligence
