#  About DoAI 
Kathmandu University Department of Artificial Intelligence (AI)
The Department of Artificial Intelligence at Kathmandu University focuses on education, research, and innovation in the field of AI. The department aims to develop skilled professionals capable of designing and deploying intelligent systems to address real-world problems. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and research programs with a curriculum that combines theoretical foundations, practical applications, and hands-on projects.
Key areas of study include machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and AI ethics. The department emphasizes interdisciplinary collaboration, preparing students to contribute to industries such as healthcare, finance, education, and technology.
With a strong focus on research, the department fosters partnerships with global institutions and industries, encouraging students to work on innovative projects and cutting-edge solutions.
